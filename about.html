Our Story

Built on a simple belief.
Every space has a story.

URHOM was founded with a simple belief — everyone deserves a beautiful, functional and inspiring space.

What started as a passion for design and quality products has today grown into a complete home and space solutions brand, serving homeowners, businesses and institutions across India.

From a single room makeover to large commercial projects, our focus remains the same — thoughtful design, premium products, and a seamless experience from inspiration to installation.
